## Browser differences
- **Lens drops** copy the canvas with `copyTexSubImage2D` (the canvas has no MSAA and no alpha) instead of the desktop's
  framebuffer blit. They're drawn on the main view only, never photos, videos, thumbnails or screenshots, as on the desktop.
- **Sound** starts after the first click or key press (the browser's autoplay rule); the muffling under a roof is a biquad
  low-pass gliding between 11 kHz and 450 Hz (the desktop: a one-pole filter).
- The lens drops' random sequence differs from the desktop's `Random(97)` (same rates and shapes).
